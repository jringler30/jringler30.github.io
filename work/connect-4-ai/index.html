Team course project · Spring 2026

Connect 4 AI

A team-built game that lets players face a CNN or Transformer model.

View repository ↗

How does the model choose a move?

Our team used self-play and Monte Carlo tree search to generate state-and-action training pairs. At inference time, a legal-move filter restricts the moves the model can return. The Anvil interface sends the board to an AWS Lightsail backend and displays the model’s move.

How the team evaluated play

The team tracked head-to-head win rates and checked model behavior through AI-versus-AI games and human playtests. The recording shows the interface during a game.
